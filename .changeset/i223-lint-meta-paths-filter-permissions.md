Add the `github-actions-paths-filter-permissions` rule and its factory `createGithubActionsPathsFilterPermissionsRule`. It reports a job that runs a paths-filter action (`dorny/paths-filter` by default, matched on the action name before `@`) when the job's effective `permissions:` does not grant `pull-requests` at `read` or `write`, the grant that action needs to read a pull request's changed files; without it the job fails with "Resource not accessible by integration". Permissions are resolved per job: a job-level block replaces the workflow-level one entirely, so a job that adds `contents: read` for itself loses a workflow-level `pull-requests: read`. Block mappings, single-line flow mappings, `{}`, `read-all` and `write-all` are read; a job with no `permissions:` at either level is reported as falling back to the repository default permissions. A flow mapping that wraps over several lines is left alone. It takes `filterActions` (default `['dorny/paths-filter']`), `workflowGlobs` (default `.github/workflows/*.y(a)ml`) and `ciCritical` (default `true`). lint-meta has no `recommended` preset, and a caller that picks factories one by one sees nothing new. The factory is part of `RULE_FACTORIES`, so `createAllRules()` now includes it: a caller of `createAllRules()` sees new violations on any filter job without `pull-requests: read`. The workflow reader also gains a per-job permissions reader, with no change in what the existing rules report.
